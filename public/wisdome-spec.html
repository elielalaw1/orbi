# Wisdome Malmö: source-backed geometry and delivery specification

Research checked against the live official Wisdome 360 site on 10 November 2026. This document separates venue-confirmed facts, the examples' coordinate conventions, and simulator approximations. It is a technical research note, not a claim that ORBI has been tested in the physical venue.

### Important unresolved venue detail

The live [venue page](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/reference/venue/#still-being-confirmed) explicitly lists **which edge of a master is the room front as still being confirmed**. The official examples and simulator assume **bottom = front**. ORBI may follow that convention, but must not describe it as a venue-confirmed orientation. Confirm at rehearsal using a front marker; retain a configurable rotation if a technician requests another edge.

The site's other listed unresolved details are laptop changeover duration and the venue's Dante sample rate. It recommends starting at 48 kHz; that is not confirmation of the actual live Dante rate.

## 2. Exact projection mapping used by EX-03

The authoritative actual source is [EX-03 Pulse](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/examples/files/EX-03-webgl-glsl/pulse.html), not just the tutorial description.

For a square master pixel normalized to `p=(x,y)` in −1…1, with **y increasing upward**:

```text
r = length(p)
if r > 1: RGB = (0,0,0)
theta = r * radians(80)
d = p/r, or (0,0) at r=0
local = (sin(theta)*d.x, -sin(theta)*d.y, cos(theta))
room = (local.x,
        local.y*cos(26°) + local.z*sin(26°),
       -local.y*sin(26°) + local.z*cos(26°))
azimuth = atan2(room.x,room.y)
elevation = asin(room.z)
```

Room axes: x right, y front, z up; azimuth 0 is audience front, negative left, positive right. Elevation 0 is eye level. `r=.5` is 40° from the dome axis. A 180° master is wrong for this venue: the complete file is squeezed by 8/9 when mapped to the venue's 160° screen.

The inverse transform uses `local.y = room.y*cos(26°) - room.z*sin(26°)` and `local.z = room.y*sin(26°) + room.z*cos(26°)`, then `r=acos(local.z)/80°`, x along local.x and master y opposite local.y. ORBI's current ProjectionMath.ts and shader roomDir agree with these formulas.

The venue ring-to-room-elevation formula straight ahead is:

```text
elevation = (90 - 26) - (1 - venueRing/90) * 160/2
sweet elevation at ring40 = 19.555555... degrees
```

Radius cancels when converting a ray to a dome-master pixel from the reference position. The 5.8 m physical radius matters for **off-center seat ray/sphere intersections**, not for changing the 160° fisheye formula.

## 3. Face geometry and curvature

The official [EX-08 flat-to-dome explanation and equations](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/examples/ex08-flat-to-dome/) distinguish correct ray projection from pasting a flat image into a fisheye:

1. Master pixel → dome direction, using 80° radial mapping.
2. Dome direction → room direction, using 26° tilt.
3. Evaluate/intersect the actual directional or virtual surface geometry.

For a planar card, use a tangent-plane ray intersection (`dot(ray,right)/dot(ray,forward)` and corresponding up coordinate), not raw fisheye pixels. This keeps straight lines straight **from the reference seat**. EX-08 recommends about 50–70° width for a flat card, and curved geometry for widths above approximately 90°. A 60° 16:9 flat card is about 36° high, not 33.75°; perspective angular dimensions are nonlinear.

ORBI is intentionally a face inhabiting the spherical screen, rather than a flat rectangular card. Its geodesic/log-tangent local face coordinates can be a legitimate curved-surface design: every feature is still evaluated from the actual room ray. It should not be advertised as a perspective-flat card or as distortion-free from every seat. The actual eyes, pupils and mouth need inspection from reference, back and side seats. A face carried over the world zenith also needs a stable tangent basis; azimuth-defined up/right vectors alone can flip there.

**No single prewarp makes an extended graphic perfectly undistorted for every seat.** EX-08 explicitly says aisle-seat bending is normal; the exact flat view is from the center. A measured seating plan or projection-system mesh is not provided by this site.

## 4. Comfort, motion, brightness and detail

[Comfortable-zone guidance](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/reference/comfortable-zone/) places important faces/text approximately within ±60° azimuth, −16°…+45° elevation, aimed near +20°. This is a content-comfort recommendation, not the physical screen boundary. User-requested motion outside that region is geometrically possible, but participants must turn or look overhead. Keep the idle/home face in the sweet spot.

The guidance recommends slow motion in degrees/second and zero global roll. Immediate input response and maximum travel speed are different controls: removing gesture debounce need not mean teleporting a large face across the dome. Rapid local facial travel should be rehearsed even though the whole scene never rotates.

Bright full-dome fields cross-reflect and reduce contrast. The [problems page](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/problems/) warns that the hemisphere can wash out to grey. Retain the requested colorful full-surface character, but keep adjustable brightness and compare the actual eyes/mouth at multiple levels, with simulated haze on/off and then with real projectors. The simulator does not measure calibrated luminance or projector black level.

At 2048², the site uses 12.8 px/degree as its practical angular detail budget; at 4096², 25.6 px/degree. Recommended cap height: 1.5° absolute minimum, 3° for body text, 6°+ for titles. Fine iris fibers or narrow glossy outlines may disappear from distant seats; readable silhouettes and broad highlights matter more.

6K renders contain nine times the pixels of 2K; the maximum resolution is not the recommended initial target. A 30 FPS delivery is required for fallback files; live stable frame rate must be measured on the installation laptop.

## 5. Simulator facts and approximations

Actual source: [Dome Simulator](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/tools/dome-simulator.html).

- `DOME_R = 5.8` metres, tilt26°, file/venue FOV160°, bottom-front convention.
- Seat positions in simulator coordinates are **explicitly approximate**, in metres:

| Seat | x | y | z |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Reference | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| Front | 0.0 | −0.9 | −2.6 |
| Middle | 0.0 | 0.2 | 0.6 |
| Back | 0.0 | 1.1 | 3.0 |
| Far left | −2.6 | 1.0 | 2.4 |

These simulator axes are not the shader's room x/right,y/front,z/up convention. Its interactive camera and sphere functions define their own basis. A symmetric right position (+2.6,1.0,2.4) is a useful ORBI test position but is not an official measured seat.

- Cross-reflection simulation adds a crude image-average term (`0.22 * average`) to projected color. It is a useful qualitative contrast test, not physical radiosity or a calibrated venue measurement.
- Screen/window/tab sharing and still/video files are actual supported inputs. Arbitrary remote WebSocket control should not be assumed.
- Shared-window cropping/title bars, mirroring and the chosen front edge affect results. Confirm source framing before evaluating distortion.
- The simulator itself states it cannot reproduce physical scale, projector brightness, 5.1 sound or 58 people seated in darkness.

## 6. Signal and fallback delivery

[Live setup](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/reference/connecting/): extend the desktop, use real 3840×2160 HDMI, centered2160² at x840,y0, circle touching top/bottom, black sides and corners. Confirm adapters have not negotiated 1080p. OBS Base and Output both3840×2160,30 FPS; square source2160² positioned840,0. OBS may add one or two frames of delay; check picture/sound sync in the room.

[Fallback file delivery](https://dcuartielles.github.io/wisdome-360/reference/delivery-spec/):

- Square2048², equidistant160° fisheye, black corners.
- Constant30 FPS; MP4, H.264 High (H.265 also accepted), 60 Mbit/s CBR or higher.
- 8-bit Rec.709.
- Audio separately, WAV48kHz/16-bit, mono files `_L _R _C _LFE _Ls _Rs`; stereo `_L _R`.
- Audio starts frame1. A1kHz frame-one tone aligns with a white flash on frame1 of the picture.
- Inspect the actual final files, completely, in the simulator. A CFR conversion cannot recover frames that were never captured.

## 7. Actionable ORBI priorities

1. Preserve verified 160°/26° ray mapping; do not add a second fisheye warp to the already directional shader.
2. Mark bottom-front as example convention, confirm it with venue technicians, and make calibration orientation adjustable if deployment requires another edge.
3. Keep home at approximately19.6–20° elevation; user-selected full-dome travel is not a reason to move idle overhead.
4. Use a stable tangent basis through world zenith and keep feature footprint inside the actual circular aperture.
5. Inspect all expressions from multiple seats; geometric mapping alone does not establish readability.
6. Separate instant input onset from travel-speed limiting. Benchmark both recognition latency and live rendering on the Mac, including the corner camera preview.
7. Preserve a completely UI-free projection output; camera preview/dashboard belong to the operator or must be explicitly hidden before sharing the projection window.
8. Treat full-dome brightness, detailed glossy shading and cross-reflection as an optical tradeoff requiring venue calibration.
9. Do not claim measured seat geometry, calibrated luminance, fixed Dante rate, finalized front orientation, hardware frame rate or venue validation from the simulator alone.
